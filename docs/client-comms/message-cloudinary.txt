PHOTO ABHI NAHI HO SAKTI - AUR USKA HAL

Ek cheez abhi nahi hoti: hotel ki photo aap khud nahi badal sakte. Filhaal
photo mujhe website ke andar daalni padti hai aur website dobara publish
karni padti hai. To har photo change ke liye aapko mujhe bolna padta hai.

Ye theek nahi hai. Isko theek karne ke liye ek account chahiye - Cloudinary.
Ye photo rakhne wali service hai.

Iske baad kya hoga:

  - Admin panel mein har hotel ke saamne "Photos" ka option aayega
  - Aap phone ya laptop se seedha photo upload kar doge
  - 10-15 second mein website pe live
  - Purani photo hatani ho to wo bhi ek click
  - Photo apne aap chhoti ho jayegi taaki phone pe website jaldi khule

Mujhe bolne ki zaroorat hi nahi padegi.
